[Supplementary Figure 4](supplemental_figures/sup_4_raw_vs_cor_metric_behaviors.pdf)
Hamming evolves less extinction and more exclusive mutualism than raw Hamming. Hamming compensates for raw Hamming's exclusionary nature. Counts of replicates are pooled across experiments 7 \& 8; we ran $5{\small,}130$ total replicates for each metric. Streak and Hash are normalized by construction; adding a normalization correction had no appreciable affect.

[Supplementary Figure 5](supplemental_figures/sup_5_tagmut_permissiveness_by_behavior.pdf)
Mean final permissiveness across permissiveness mutation effect sizes split by behavior for experiment 6. Proportion of replicates that evolved each behavior in experiment 6 with mean final host permissiveness values at differing levels of permissiveness mutation size across behaviors: coexistence (a), exclusive mutualism (b), exclusive parasitism (c), and symbiont extinction (d). Mean final permissiveness is collected from the last update in which a surviving symbiont exists; for non-extinct symbiont populations, this update is the last of the experiment. We record mean final permissiveness because permissiveness undergoes drift after symbionts go extinct. Hosts began the experiments with permissiveness 0.375. Organisms start the experiment with all-0 tags. Organisms start as all parasites and we vary tag mutation (TM) rate.

[Supplementary Figure 6](supplemental_figures/sup_6_vt_permissiveness_by_behavior.pdf)
Mean final permissiveness across permissiveness mutation effect sizes split by behavior for experiment 5. Proportion of replicates that evolved each behavior in experiment 5 with mean final host permissiveness values at differing levels of permissiveness mutation size across behaviors: coexistence (a), exclusive mutualism (b), exclusive parasitism (c), and symbiont extinction (d). Mean final permissiveness is collected from the last update in which a surviving symbiont exists; for non-extinct symbiont populations, this update is the last of the experiment. We record mean final permissiveness because permissiveness undergoes drift after symbionts go extinct. Hosts began the experiments with permissiveness 0.375. Organisms start the experiment with all-0 tags. Organisms start as a mix of mutualists and parasites and we vary vertical transmission (VT) rate.
